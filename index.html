Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some things our clients often ask. Have more questions? Reach out via our contact section.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking an appointment to secure your preferred date and time, especially for hair services. Walk-ins are welcome for product purchases and may be accommodated for services depending on availability.

What hair types do your stylists work with?

Our stylists work with all hair types — 1A through 4C. Whether you have fine, straight hair or thick, coily natural hair, we have the expertise and products to give you the best result.

Can I bring my own hair products or extensions?

Yes, you are welcome to bring your own hair or extensions for installation services. We also carry a range of premium hair products in-store if you would prefer to purchase from us.

How long does a full wig installation take?

A typical wig installation takes between 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the wig type, the method used (glued vs. glue-free), and any additional styling required. We'll give you an estimated duration when you book.

Are the beauty products you sell genuine / authentic?

Yes. We only stock and sell products that have been quality-checked and sourced through reputable suppliers. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities.

Do you offer consultations before a service?

Absolutely. We offer brief consultations before every service to understand your hair goals, assess your hair's condition, and recommend the best approach. There is no additional charge for consultations.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept cash and bank transfers. Please confirm current payment options when you get in touch, as these may change. We will always inform you of accepted methods when you book.
